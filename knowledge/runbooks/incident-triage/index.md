# Reasoning about a household condition

Portable procedure for an agent that meets a condition it did not cause. It
answers *in what order do I work?*, *what may I conclude from what I saw?*, and
*what do I record about what I tried?*

This is **context, not authority**. Reasoning about a condition is not authority
over it, and nothing here grants capability, authorization, safety approval, or
any power to remediate.

| what may be concluded, and what stays unknown | [evidence.md](evidence.md) |
