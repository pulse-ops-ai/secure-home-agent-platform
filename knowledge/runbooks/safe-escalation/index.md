# Stopping, and handing over

Portable procedure for the point at which an agent stops reasoning and gives a
situation to a person. It answers *when must I stop?* and *what does a good
handover carry?*

This is **context, not authority**. Stopping is not a route to anything an agent
could not already do, and this document names no person and no way of reaching
one.
